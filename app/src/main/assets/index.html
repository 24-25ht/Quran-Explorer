Tajweed Color Rules Guide

Color legend used in Basic Rules and Full Color reading modes.
Ghunnah / Iqlab — Nasalized pronunciation held for 2 counts on Noon or Meem Mushaddadah and Iqlab transitions.
Ikhfa & Ikhfa Shafawi — Light nasal concealment when Noon Sakinah or Meem Sakinah meets concealed letters.
Idgham — Smooth merging/assimilation of adjacent letters with or without nasal resonance.
Qalqalah — Crisp echoing resonance on Qaf, Ta, Ba, Jim, and Dal when resting with Sukoon.
Madd Elongations — Natural (2 counts), Permissible (4-5 counts), and Necessary (6 counts) vowel prolongation.
Lam Shamsiyah & Silent Letters — Unpronounced letters absorbed smoothly during connected recitation (Full Color mode).

Version History & Release Notes

Complete release archive for Quran Explorer (v1.0 to v3.5)
Current Release v3.5 (Build 24)

Sifr Mistadir Rendering Fix, Universal Ayah Numbering & Selection-Lock Prevention (Gen 3 Finale)

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Sifr Mistadir (U+06DF) Multi-Script & Tajweed-Off Fix: Resolved the outlined dotted-circle rendering defect on silent letters (e.g., كَفَرُوا۟) caused by KFGQPC Hafs mapping U+06DF to a standalone dotted circle and IndoPak Nastaleeq leaving it blank. Added normalizeFontMarksForScript plus Tajweed-off cluster shaping in applyClientTajweedHtml so Sifr Mistadir renders cleanly across Uthmani, IndoPak, Imla'i, Search, and Word-by-Word whether Tajweed is enabled or disabled.
  • Universal Ayah End-Numbering Across All Surahs: Added ornate verse ending markers (﴿1﴾–﴿7﴾) across curated Surahs (Al-Fatihah, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas, Ayat al-Kursi) and introduced a universal ensureVerseEndingNumber guard in renderVerseArabicHtml so every Surah and hydrated Ayah displays its end marker reliably across all scripts.
  • Unselectable Reader Text (No Sticky Selection Lock): Disabled accidental WebView text selection and long-press callouts across Arabic verses, translations, Tafsir panels, and Word-by-Word cards while keeping search and filter inputs editable, preventing Android hardware Back lockups.
  • Complete Generation 3 (v3.0–v3.5) Archive: Expanded in-app, GitHub, and Markdown release notes to cover v3.4 and v3.5 alongside one-tap Generation 3 summary copying.
v3.4 (Build 23)

Audio Bar Cleanup & Android Media Notification Card Polish

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Removed Cluttered Offline/Stream Badge: Removed the redundant status pill from the compact bottom audio bar to give Surah titles, verse indicators, and Qari names full breathing room on mobile widths.
  • Media Notification Player Card Styling: Upgraded the bottom audio bar with sleek frosted elevation, layered depth gradients, and tactile circular playback controls inspired by native Android lockscreen/notification media widgets.
v3.3 (Build 22)

Bundled Quranic Arabic Fonts, Full Script Switching & Multi-Script Tajweed

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Bundled Static Arabic Fonts (Zero Missing Glyphs): Replaced the Latin-only Amiri stub and bundled full static Uthmanic Hafs, IndoPak Nastaleeq (with PUA waqf symbols), Noto Naskh Arabic, and full Arabic Amiri inside res/font/ with @font-face rules so Quranic marks, madd symbols, and IndoPak letters never fall back to system glyphs or broken boxes offline.
  • True Uthmani, IndoPak & Imla'i Switching: Fixed an issue where Uthmani Tajweed markup overrode IndoPak and Imla'i script selections, synced data-script CSS variables across Reader, Bismillah, Search, Tafsir, Settings Preview, and Word-by-Word, and hydrated full-verse script variants alongside Tajweed data.
  • Universal Multi-Script Tajweed Engine: Expanded API v4 verse hydration with text_uthmani_tajweed, added cluster-safe sanitization and client-side Tajweed highlighting across Uthmani, IndoPak, Imla'i, and Word-by-Word tiles without breaking Arabic cursive ligatures.
v3.1 (Build 20)

Full Settings Hub, Decluttered Prayer Timetable & Word-by-Word Sync

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Word-by-Word Verse Sync & Authentic Hydration: Fixed Surah/Ayah switching getting stuck or showing mismatched text vs. audio, synced bottom player state with the active Word-by-Word verse, added Prev/Next Ayah buttons, and added IndexedDB v5 verse caching for authentic per-word breakdown across all 114 Surahs.
  • Decluttered Prayer Times + Mini Settings Panel: Placed Next Prayer Countdown and daily schedule cleanly at the top with zero scrolling, fixed location header overlap, and moved GPS, country/city presets, convention, and Asr school into a Prayer-only collapsible mini settings panel that auto-opens on first visit.
  • Complete Settings Screen: Added a dedicated Settings page in the Sidebar and Quick Settings with live typography preview, Arabic/Translation font size sliders, script selector, Tajweed modes, Default Tafsir edition, Default Qari, playback speed, auto-play next Ayah, and Hifz repeat defaults.
v3.0 (Build 19)

Alphabetical Tafsir Library (Bangla & Multilingual), Temporary GPS Prayer Times & Espresso Sepia Polish

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Word-by-Word Containment & Root Theme Continuity: Fixed word tile overflow across narrow screens and font scaling, and mirrored theme and reading comfort tokens to the document root so short views never reveal a dark bottom seam.
  • Soft Warm Espresso Sepia Mode: Calibrated Dark + Warm Sepia eye comfort with warm espresso surfaces, sage-emerald accents, and gold-taupe hairline borders and inner card lining.
  • Downloads -> Multilingual Tafsir Library: Added an alphabetically grouped Tafsir catalog (Arabic, Bangla, English, Indonesian, Kurdish, Russian, Urdu) with surah-by-surah IndexedDB caching, progress/pause/retry, and direct Quran Reader & Tafsir viewer integration.
  • Global & Regional Prayer Times + Temporary GPS: Fixed solar prayer time calculation math, added single-shot city-level Android GPS area detection (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION with immediate stop), and added presets for Bangladesh (IFB), India (Karachi), Indonesia (KEMENAG), Qatar, and international regions with Hanafi/Standard Asr toggles.
v2.5 (Build 18)

Obsidian-Emerald Theme Overhaul, Quick Settings Fix, Juz/Hizb Jump & Reading Comfort

Highlights & Improvements:
  • Obsidian-Emerald & Warm Alabaster Theme: Redesigned color tokens with crisp hierarchy, emerald primary highlights, champagne-gold accents, and cohesive light theme surfaces.
  • Cohesive Splash Screen: Upgraded Android Compose launch splash and HTML load veil with an 8-point Rub el Hizb crest for smooth startup transitions.
  • Quick Settings Overlay Fix: Detached the Quick Settings drawer into a top-level body layer so it opens cleanly without clipping inside the header bar, adding direct playback speed controls (0.75x to 1.5x).

  • Search Surahs, Ayahs & Smart Filters: Plain Surah search paired with Ayah keyword matching, split cleanly into separate Surah and Ayah result sections with Scope (All, Surahs, Ayahs, Filter Out All) and Origin (Meccan, Medinan) chips.
  • Juz (30), Hizb (60) & Page (1-604) Navigator: Tabbed navigator supporting direct jumps to every Juz, Hizb, and Mushaf page alongside compact bottom-left Juz · Hizb · Page metadata on verses.
  • Tool & Preview Fixes: Repaired Downloads tabs/filters, Word-by-Word audio rate and batch sync, Tafsir commentary switcher (Ibn Kathir, Jalalayn, As-Saadi), Qatar live next-prayer countdown, and upgraded Compose Previews.
